> **This is a sample post.** It exists to show you the structure. Delete it
> before you launch, and copy this file as a starting template for real reports.

## Summary

Open with 2–3 sentences stating the verifiable facts: who, what is alleged or
documented, by which authority, and the current legal status. Keep it neutral.

## What the records show

Lay out the facts in order, **and cite as you go**. Good, safe phrasing looks
like this:

- "According to the FIR registered on [date]..." 
- "The agency's report states that..." 
- "The High Court, in its order dated [date], observed that..."

Avoid stating guilt as fact. Write *"is alleged to have"* / *"faces charges of"*
rather than *"is guilty of"* — unless there is an actual conviction, in which
case say *"was convicted of … by [court] on [date]"* and link the judgment.

## The person's response

If the subject or their party has responded, summarise it here and link it.
If they declined or did not respond, say so plainly. This fairness step is a
strong legal protection.

## Why it matters

Brief, clearly-flagged analysis or context. Make clear this part is informed
commentary, not new factual allegation.
