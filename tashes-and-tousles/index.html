The name, explained

A tash is engineered. A tousle is coaxed.

We never understood why those two things needed separate doors. One room, two disciplines, no assumptions about who sits in which chair.

Tashes

Clipper work, scissor-over-comb, straight-razor finishes and beards mapped to the face they actually sit on. Hot towel, cold steel, no rush.

Tousles

Cuts that keep their shape as they grow out, colour read in Ahmedabad daylight rather than salon bulbs, and finishes built for a humid afternoon.

The room

Off CG Road, above the noise.

Ahmedabad spent a century weaving thread into cloth, which is a decent way to think about hair: tension, direction, and knowing when to stop pulling.

The room is eight chairs, brass fittings, and a lot of natural light on the west wall — which is why we check colour by the window before you leave, never under a spotlight.

Walk-ins welcome before 12:00. After that the chairs are usually spoken for, so book ahead.
